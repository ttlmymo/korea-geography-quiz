About Guro-gu

Located at the southwestern gateway to Seoul, this autonomous district is home to the Seoul Digital Industrial Complex—a high-tech venture hub that emerged from the former Guro Industrial Complex. It is also known as a multicultural commercial district where diverse cultures blend together on the flat terrain through which the Anyangcheon and Mokgamcheon streams flow.

History of Guro-gu

During the Three Kingdoms period, this area was the site of continuous struggles among Goguryeo, Baekje, and Silla over control of the Han River basin. During the Goryeo Dynasty, it served as the stronghold of the Geumju Kang clan, descendants of Gang Gam-chan. During the Joseon Dynasty, it was a rural area belonging to Geumcheon County and the Bupyeong Provincial Office, and in 1963, it was incorporated into Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul. In 1980, centered around the Guro Industrial Complex, it was separated from Yeongdeungpo-gu to form the new Guro-gu, and in 1995, the areas of Doksan-dong and Siheung-dong were split off to establish Geumcheon-gu.

People & Stories

In Cheonwang-dong, a legend is passed down about Prime Minister Ha Yeon, who served as Chief State Councilor during the reign of King Sejong. It is said that his spirit appeared to the new district magistrate, asking him to stop tomb raiders who had entered his burial site. The courageous magistrate granted his request, saving the prime minister’s spirit and even securing a royal pardon. In addition, the legend of the zelkova tree in Gaebong-dong remains, and since the area was a rural village from ancient times, Dodaengje rituals—praying for the village’s safety and a bountiful harvest—were widely practiced throughout the area every Full Moon of the First Lunar Month.
