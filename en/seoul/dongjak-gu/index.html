About Dongjak-gu

Located in southwestern Seoul, across the Han River from Yongsan and Yeouido, this autonomous district has long served as a key transportation hub connecting the Samnam region with the capital via the Nodul Ferry (Noryangjin). It is a land of loyalty and filial piety, home to the Seoul National Cemetery and the Tombs of the Six Loyal Ministers, and is also widely known for the Noryangjin Fish Market and its academic district.

History of Dongjak-gu

During the Three Kingdoms period, the area alternated between the territories of Baekje, Goguryeo, and Silla. The fact that it was named ‘Jin’ (meaning ‘ferry’) during the Unified Silla period indicates that a ferry crossing had already been established in the Noryangjin area. During the Joseon Dynasty, the area was divided between Gwacheon County and Siheung (formerly Geumcheon), and a village developed along the Samnam-daero road, which crossed the Han River at Dongjakjin and led to Namtaeryeong. Although the ferry’s function disappeared with the construction of the Han River Bridge in 1900 and the opening of the Gyeongin Railway, the area transformed into a key hub for modern transportation. After liberation, the area was part of Yeongdeungpo-gu and Gwanak-gu before being separated from Gwanak-gu in 1980 to form the newly established Dongjak-gu.

People & Stories

In Noryangjin Bon-dong, there is the Tomb of the Six Loyal Ministers, which enshrines Seong Sam-mun and the other five ministers who died a martyr’s death while attempting to restore King Danjong to the throne; memorial rites are held there every year to honor their loyalty. The National Seoul Cemetery in Dongjak-dong is the final resting place for patriotic martyrs and national heroes who dedicated their lives to the country, making it a place of remembrance visited by people from all over the nation. There is also a poignant legend that a woman, who had been deeply in love with Park Tae-bo—who died opposing the dethronement of Queen Inhyeon—took her own life on the day Nogang Seowon was completed.
