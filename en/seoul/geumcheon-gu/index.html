Geumcheon-gu, Seoul

About Geumcheon-gu

Located at the southwestern tip of Seoul along the Anyang Stream, this autonomous district was once the center of the former Siheung County. The Gasan and Doksan-dong areas were developed into the Seoul Digital Industrial Complex (the successor to the Guro Industrial Complex) and have grown into one of Seoul’s leading industrial hubs, home to a high concentration of cutting-edge IT and venture companies.

History of Geumcheon-gu

In ancient times, this area was part of the Mahan and Baekje kingdoms. After a period of rivalry among the Three Kingdoms, it was known as Gogyang County during the Unified Silla period and as Geumju (衿州) during the Goryeo period; the name ‘Geumcheon (衿川)’ also originated from this. In particular, the Geumcheon-gu area has been the stronghold of the Geumju Kang clan—descendants of Gang Gam-chan—ever since his ancestors settled there. During the Joseon Dynasty, it belonged to Siheung County and remained a rural area on the outskirts of Seoul for a long time. In 1963, it was incorporated into Seoul and became part of Yeongdeungpo District. Subsequently, in 1980, it was transferred to Guro District, and in 1995, Geumcheon District was newly established by separating Gasan, Doksan, and Siheung-dong from Guro District. Today, the former Guro Industrial Complex has been transformed into the Seoul Digital Industrial Complex.

People & Stories

Hoam Mountain in Siheung-dong is home to Hoam Mountain Fortress, said to have been built during the Unified Silla period, demonstrating that the area was a key military stronghold guarding the lower reaches of the Han River. At the foot of the mountain lies Hoapsa Temple, which is said to have been built by King Taejo Yi Seong-gye to suppress the 'fire energy' (hwagi) of Hanyang; legend has it that the temple was constructed at the tail of the tiger-shaped mountain peak to subdue that energy. In addition, several feng shui legends are passed down in this area regarding the selection of the capital at the beginning of the Joseon Dynasty.
