Yangcheon-gu, Seoul

About Yangcheon-gu

This autonomous district is located in the lowlands of the Gimpo Plain in southwestern Seoul, where the Anyangcheon Stream meets the Han River. It has grown into a media hub with a high concentration of broadcasting companies, as well as the large-scale apartment complexes and private tutoring districts of the Mok-dong New Town, and has reclaimed its former name, ‘Yangcheon.’

History of Yangcheon-gu

During the Three Kingdoms period, it served as a strategic military stronghold along the lower Han River, as evidenced by remaining ruins such as the earthen fortress in Sinjeong-dong. It became Gongam-hyeon during the Unified Silla period and was named ‘Yangcheon (陽川)’ during the reign of King Chungseon of Goryeo. During the Joseon Dynasty, it functioned as Yangcheon County, governed by a fifth-rank county magistrate, and remained an independent administrative unit for a long time; however, its name disappeared when it was incorporated into Gimpo County following the Japanese colonial government’s administrative reorganization in 1914. After being incorporated into Seoul in 1963, it was part of Yeongdeungpo-gu and Gangseo-gu; when the population surged due to the development of the Mok-dong New Town, it was separated from Gangseo-gu in 1988, and Yangcheon-gu was established. Thus, after 74 years, the area regained the old name 'Yangcheon' that it had lost in 1914.

People & Stories

Yangcheon Hyanggyo, located next to the former Yangcheon County government office, is the only hyanggyo in Seoul. Although it is now part of Gangseo-gu, it is a heritage site that shares the roots of the name ‘Yangcheon.’ In Sinjeong-dong, Samhaeju—a precious liquor brewed in three stages—is preserved as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Seoul, and in Mok-dong, the craftsmanship of Chogojang, a traditional weaving workshop specializing in patterned stone mats, continues to this day. Additionally, in the Gomdalle Village of Sinwol-dong, a poignant legend is passed down about a woman who lost her life while waiting for her beloved during the era of conflict between Silla and Baekje.
