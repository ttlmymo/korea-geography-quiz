About Gangbuk-gu

Located at the northeastern tip of Seoul, this autonomous district is set against the backdrop of Mount Bukhan—Seoul’s sacred mountain—and consists mostly of pleasant residential areas. It is well known as a region where nature and modern history blend harmoniously, home to the Ui-dong Valley, renowned temples such as Doseonsa and Hwagye Temple, and the National April 19 Democratic Cemetery.

History of Gangbuk-gu

During the Three Kingdoms period, it was a strategic stronghold over which Baekje, Goguryeo, and Silla vied for control of Mount Bukhan; King Jinheung designated Mount Bukhan as Silla’s border and erected a commemorative stele. During the Joseon Dynasty, the area belonged to Yangju, outside the capital’s walls. After enduring the Imjin War and the Manchu Invasion, the present-day Bukhansan Fortress was constructed during the reign of King Sukjong to serve as both a refuge and a defensive stronghold. After liberation, the area belonged to Dobong-gu via Seongbuk-gu, but as the population grew significantly, Mia-dong, Beon-dong, Suyu-dong, and Ui-dong were separated from Dobong-gu in 1995 to establish the new Gangbuk-gu. For this reason, it is still often grouped together with Dobong-gu and Nowon-gu as the ‘northeastern region.’

People & Stories

Doseonsa Temple in Ui-dong is said to have been founded by National Master Doseon during the late Silla period, while Hwagye Temple still houses a beautiful bronze bell and the Daeungjeon Hall from the late Joseon Dynasty. The National April 19 Democratic Cemetery in Suyu-dong is the final resting place for those who sacrificed their lives during the April 19 Revolution, attracting visitors every year to pay their respects. Additionally, an inscription of 69 characters embodying the spirit of the March 1st Movement remains carved into a rock at the summit of Baegunbong Peak on Mount Bukhansan, making this area a symbolic site of national spirit. The Mia-dong area is also home to the legend of Turtle Rock, said to have originated from a battle between the Turtle Rock and mountain spirits.
