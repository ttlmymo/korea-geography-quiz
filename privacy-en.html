Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026 · 한국어

Korea Geography Master Quiz (the "Service") values the privacy of its users and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what personal information the Service collects and how it is used and stored.

1. Information We Collect

The Service collects the following information when you sign in, in order to provide the leaderboard feature.

  • Google sign-in: account identifier (user ID), email address, profile name
  • Kakao sign-in: account identifier (user ID), profile nickname
  • The leaderboard nickname you enter yourself
  • Service usage records such as game scores and number of plays

The following information may also be collected automatically while you use the Service.

  • Access logs, device and browser information, and usage statistics collected through cookies and similar technologies (Google Analytics)
  • Certain settings such as your high score and nickname are stored only in your browser's local storage and are not transmitted to our servers.

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Member identification and sign-in functionality
  • Registering and displaying leaderboard rankings
  • Analyzing usage statistics and improving the Service
  • Operating the Service and preventing abuse

3. Retention and Use Period

As a rule, the Service destroys personal information without delay once the purpose of its collection and use has been achieved. Specific retention periods are as follows.

  • Account (sign-in) information and leaderboard data: retained until you withdraw your membership (delete your account) or request deletion, and destroyed without delay upon such request.
  • Usage statistics collected via Google Analytics: retained for up to 14 months and then automatically deleted.

If you sign out or disconnect your social account, processing of the related information stops. When an account is deleted, the leaderboard information stored for it is automatically deleted as well.

4. Entrustment of Processing to Third Parties

The Service uses the following external services for its operation, and some information may be processed in that context.

  • Supabase: sign-in authentication and leaderboard data storage
  • Google / Kakao: social sign-in authentication
  • Google Analytics: collection and analysis of usage statistics
  • Google AdSense: serving advertisements
  • NAVER Maps: map display

5. Advertising and Cookies

The Service uses cookies and similar technologies for usage analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense). Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve interest-based ads to users.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • For more about how third-party vendors use cookies, see Google's advertising policies.
  • You may refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some features may then be unavailable.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time, and you may withdraw your consent to collection by signing out or disconnecting your social account.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at:

  • Email: koquiz.support@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised in line with changes to applicable laws or the Service. Any changes will be announced on this page.
